# Incident Response Plan

## Scope and roles

This plan covers security incidents affecting company systems or customer data. The Security Operations team leads response, supported by an on-call incident commander available 24x7.

## Detection and triage

Incidents are detected through SIEM alerting, endpoint detection and response, and employee reports. Every alert is triaged and assigned a severity from SEV1 to SEV4.

## Customer notification

Affected customers are notified of a confirmed security breach involving their data within 72 hours of confirmation.

## Testing the plan

The incident response plan is tested at least annually through tabletop exercises, and lessons learned are tracked to closure.

## Post-incident review

A blameless post-incident review is completed for every SEV1 and SEV2 incident within 10 business days.
