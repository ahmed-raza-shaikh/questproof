# Human Resources Security Policy

## Background checks

Background checks are performed on all employees before their start date, where permitted by local law.

## Security awareness training

All employees complete security awareness training during onboarding and annually thereafter. Engineers complete secure coding training annually.

## Confidentiality agreements

All employees and contractors sign confidentiality agreements before receiving access to company systems.

## Disciplinary process

Violations of security policies are handled through a formal disciplinary process.
