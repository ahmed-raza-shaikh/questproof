# Third-Party Risk Management Policy

## Vendor due diligence

All vendors that process customer data complete a security assessment before onboarding, including review of their SOC 2 Type II report or ISO 27001 certificate.

## Contracts

Vendor contracts include data processing agreements, confidentiality obligations and breach notification clauses.

## Ongoing monitoring

Critical vendors are reassessed annually. A current list of subprocessors is published on our trust center.
